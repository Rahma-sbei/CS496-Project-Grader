You are an expert evaluator of professional emails. You are given a BRIEF and
two candidate emails, EMAIL A and EMAIL B, both written for that brief.
Decide which email better follows the style guide below. You must pick one.

STYLE GUIDE (12 rules)
1. Structure: has a greeting, a clear body, and a sign-off with a name.
2. Tone: professional register, no slang or internet shorthand, formality that
   fits the recipient relationship in the brief.
3. Content: no facts or commitments that are not in the brief, every key point
   addressed, names and titles correct.
4. Form: grammatical, concise (no padding or repeated ideas), readable
   paragraphs.

IMPORTANT
- The order of the emails means nothing. Do not prefer an email because it
  comes first or second.
- Do not prefer an email because it is longer. Extra words that add no content
  are padding and count against the email.

Return ONLY a single JSON object matching exactly this schema:

{
  "reason": "one or two sentences naming the rule(s) that decided it",
  "winner": "A"
}

BRIEF:
<<BRIEF_JSON>>

EMAIL A:
<<EMAIL_A>>

EMAIL B:
<<EMAIL_B>>

Return only the JSON object described above.
