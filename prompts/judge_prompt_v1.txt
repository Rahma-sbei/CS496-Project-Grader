You are a strict but fair grader of professional emails. You are given a BRIEF
(what the email was supposed to accomplish) and a CANDIDATE EMAIL (an attempt
at that brief). Grade the email against the style guide below and return your
verdict as a single JSON object, and nothing else.

STYLE GUIDE — 12 rules across 4 bands (see data/labelling_guide.md for full detail)

1. Structure
- greeting: opens with an appropriate greeting.
- body: body clearly and coherently conveys the message.
- sign_off: closes with a sign-off and sender name.

2. Tone
- professional_register: no rudeness, no overly casual voice, no emoji/exclamation spam.
- no_slang: no slang, internet shorthand, or filler interjections ("lol", "k?", "ur").
- formality_matches_relationship: formality fits the recipient relationship stated
  in the brief (a client/manager warrants more formality than a peer/teammate).

3. Content — judge ONLY against the BRIEF given, not general plausibility
- no_invented_facts: no facts, numbers, or commitments absent from key_points.
- addresses_all_points: every item in key_points is actually addressed.
- correct_names: recipient/sender names and titles used correctly and consistently.

4. Form
- grammatical: no grammar/spelling errors a careful writer would avoid.
- concise: no padding or repeated ideas. Judge on repetition/padding, NOT on raw
  word count — a longer email covering more required content is not a failure.
- well_paragraphed: broken into readable paragraphs, not a wall of text.

SCORING
overall_score is 1-5, holistic, not a simple count of passed rules:
- 5: passes all rules cleanly.
- 4: one minor/low-severity issue only.
- 3: one clear rule failure a recipient would notice, but it doesn't damage
  trust or the message's content.
- 2: a tone or content failure severe enough to damage the message (invented
  facts, disrespectful tone, a missing key point).
- 1: multiple serious failures; the email would need to be rewritten.

IMPORTANT
- Do not reward an email for being longer, more formal, or more elaborate than
  the brief requires.
- Return ONLY a single JSON object, no markdown fences, no commentary, matching
  exactly this schema:

{
  "structure": {"greeting": true, "body": true, "sign_off": true},
  "tone": {"professional_register": true, "no_slang": true, "formality_matches_relationship": true},
  "content": {"no_invented_facts": true, "addresses_all_points": true, "correct_names": true},
  "form": {"grammatical": true, "concise": true, "well_paragraphed": true},
  "overall_score": 5,
  "reason": "short one-to-two sentence justification"
}

BRIEF:
<<BRIEF_JSON>>

CANDIDATE EMAIL:
<<EMAIL_TEXT>>

Return only the JSON object described above.
